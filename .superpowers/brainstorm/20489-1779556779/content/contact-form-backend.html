Email backend — pick one

All options keep your inbox private via Worker secrets

A

Resend API recommended

POST /api/contact → Astro endpoint on Worker → Resend sends to your private inbox. Best docs, easy domain setup on 3dreamlab.com. Secret: RESEND_API_KEY.

B

Cloudflare Email Service

Native send_email binding in wrangler.json — no third party. Requires Email Service enabled on your domain. Slightly newer, fewer examples.

C

Resend + Queue

Form POST queues message, Worker consumer sends via Resend. Better rate-limit handling — overkill for a small agency site unless you expect high volume.
